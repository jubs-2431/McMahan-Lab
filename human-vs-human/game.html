How the Game Works

You and your opponent will play 7 rounds. Each round has a fresh pot of $100.

  • Both choose Split → you each get $50.
  • You choose Steal, opponent chooses Split → you get $100, they get nothing.
  • You choose Split, opponent chooses Steal → you get $0, they get $100.
  • Both choose Steal → you both get $0.

For each round, you have 30 seconds to discuss and make your choice. After that, there is a 5-second transition before the next round starts.

Your Choice

During the 30-second choice window, pick Split or Steal. Once you choose, it cannot be changed.
