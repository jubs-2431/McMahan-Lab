How to Play Split or Steal

You will play 7 rounds. Each round has a fresh $100 pot. Talk with the avatar, then choose Split or Steal.

  • Once you submit Split or Steal for a round, your choice cannot be changed.
  • There are no right or wrong choices. Choose naturally.
